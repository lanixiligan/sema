# Sema: Development Plan

## What Sema is

Sema is a browser-only ASL fingerspelling coach (English A–Z). It checks a letter's handshape from 21 hand points, found with MediaPipe in the webcam picture; video never leaves the device.
It's a portfolio project by Lanix Iligan, a CS student, built in stages: webcam, hand tracking, letter recognition, then the learning app.
Filipino Sign Language comes only after the American version is finalized.

## Phases

- Phase 0, render the webcam: done.
- Phase 1, hand tracking: done (deployed on Vercel).
- Phase 2, recognition (the 24 static letters): done (2026-10-23, merged into `main`). The recorder (2.1) is done (2026-10-18; built on `phase-2-1-recorder`, merged into `main`); Lanix recorded a first test session on 2026-10-20 (the 8 letters, both hands). Training (2.2) is done: `/dev/extract` turns public datasets' photos into hand points, and `training/` cleans them (`training/reports/cleaning-asl-hg.md`, `cleaning-aslyset.md`), then tests a k-NN (`training/reports/knn.md`) and two small models (`training/reports/small.md`). Logistic regression does best and is the model in the browser (14 KB, `public/models/asl-letters.json`): trained on ASL-HG and ASLYset (14 signers), 94.7% on ASL-HG's signers when each is held out (91.8% on ASL-HG alone; 18 of 24 letters at 90%+), and all 8 of Lanix's recorded letters on both hands. R, U, X, T and N (82–85%) are the weakest. The live check (2.3) is done: Try (`/try`, on the live site too), renamed Practice (`/practice`) on 2026-10-23 and merged into Learn (`/learn`) on 2026-10-27. Sema checks the letter the person picked (see "Target letter only" below) and passes it after a 1.5-second hold while the hand meets the letter's finger conditions (until 2026-10-27, a 3-second hold while it was the model's sure guess; see "Finger conditions decide" below). The finger hints (2.4) moved to Phase 3, with Learn's lessons. The model keeps improving while the next phases go on (more datasets, better hand measurements), judged by the same held-out tests.
- Phase 3, the designed pages: done for now (2026-10-27, Lanix's call). What's left is added along the way: saving progress (per-letter status and Reset progress, the rest of 3.2; Learn and Quiz save nothing yet), then what Next letter picks, the teaching order and letter states in the guide, which depend on it; the 3.3 test with new users; and a keyboard, contrast and screen-reader pass (3.5). The shell and setup (3.1) were built early, with the recorder, and are done (2026-10-18). Practice (3.4) was Try, renamed and laid out like Record (2026-10-23), and was merged into Learn on 2026-10-27 (see "Learn absorbed Practice" below). Quiz was added on 2026-10-27 at Lanix's request (letters or words from memory, no hints for now). Learn (3.3) was started on 2026-10-27, on `phase-3-preview`: one lesson per letter, A to Z, each with the camera, the letter's drawing and the live finger checklist (until later on 2026-10-27 also its steps from `letters.json`, which said the same as the checklist), a 1.5-second hold to pass (3 s until 2026-10-27) and a button to the next letter; nothing is saved yet and the teaching order is plain A to Z. The finger hints (from 2.4: which finger to fix, for a shape that doesn't pass) are Learn's finger checklist. B's came early (2026-10-23), because the model called an open hand B: shape rules on top of the model (`src/vision/landmarks/letterShapes.js`), whose failed rule was the hint. Every letter's rules came with Learn (2026-10-27), and they now decide alone (see "Finger conditions decide" below).

## Decisions made

- ASL fingerspelling, English A–Z. Say "ASL fingerspelling", never just "sign language". Sema matches shapes; never say it understands signs.
- Letters (2026-10-22; before, the first 8 alone, then the others in stages): the classifier is trained on all 24 static letters (A–Z but J and Z) from the start, since the training photos have them all. All 24 could be picked from 2026-10-23 (on Practice; all 26 on Learn now, J and Z traced); before, letters were to be switched on in stages as each passed the test in 2.2, and the weaker ones improve as the model does; the fist letters (A, E, M, N, S, T) and the sideways ones (G, H, P, Q) are expected to be the hardest. B, C, D, F, I, L, W, Y are also tested on Lanix's own recordings. J and Z move, so they come last: the first version of Sema is the 24 static letters, and J and Z are out of its scope until Phase 4 (2026-10-22). Training datasets only need the 24 (ASLYset has no J or Z).
- Target letter only (2026-10-23; before, "not sure" for hands that aren't a letter, tested on "none" recordings, and before that a "no letter" class): Sema always knows which letter it asked for (Learn, the quiz) and only checks whether the hand is that letter, so there is no "none" anywhere, the recorder included. Sema doesn't read free fingerspelling with no target. "Not sure" stays: a guess under the confidence threshold never counts (and since 2026-10-27 no guess passes a letter; see "Finger conditions decide"). Old sessions' "none" takes are skipped by training.
- Training data (2026-10-23; before, ASL-HG alone): public photos are the training data, and Lanix's own recordings the test. Since 2026-10-23 that's ASL-HG (10 signers) and ASLYset (4 signers), both CC BY 4.0 and credited in `docs/ATTRIBUTION.md` and the README. A dataset is added only if it raises the held-out score of the signers that were there before it (ASLYset: ASL-HG's signers 91.8% → 94.7%); other candidates (American Hand Signs, SignAlphaSet, Cabana's) don't say who signed each photo, so they could only train, never test. Training datasets only need the 24 static letters. Results across people come from holding out one signer at a time, never from a random split.
- Browser only: no backend, database or accounts until Phase 5. Recorded hand points stay private in `data/raw/`.
- Desktop first. Phone layouts aren't a goal yet.
- React, Vite, plain JavaScript and plain CSS. No new dependencies without asking Lanix. Python runs only offline, to train the classifier.
- Setup is Welcome's Get started, which opens the camera check, then Learn (2026-10-27: Get started briefly opened Learn directly, and Lanix put the camera check back the same day; before, the three intro pages came first). The camera check makes sure Sema sees one hand, left or right, whole (wrist in view) for 3 seconds (until 2026-10-31 it needed both hands together). Setup doesn't ask which hand the user signs with (2026-10-18; before, a Signing hand page asked): the signing hand starts as Right and is switched with the hand toggle in the camera controls. Left hands are flipped to look like right hands before classifying.
- Switching Left/Right lives in the camera controls of each camera view (2026-10-16). On Record it picks the hand for the next take; on Learn and the quiz (since 2026-10-23, first on Practice) it changes the setting.
- Hosting is Vercel. About explains how Sema handles your data (its third section; a page of its own, `/privacy`, until 2026-10-27), but there's no cookie banner, because there are no analytics or tracking.
- Record is a developer tool (2026-10-22; before, the main page at `/record`, where setup led and returning visitors started): `/dev/record`, behind the access code, now that the classifier is trained and recording is only for the test set. Lanix gives the code to the people who record. Setup and returning visitors went to Learn, a placeholder then, and from the same day to Try (renamed Practice, then merged into Learn; below).
- Developer tools (2026-10-22): on the live site too, under `/dev`, behind an access code that is a keep-out sign checked in the browser (no server), not a lock. The classifier page left `/dev/try` the same day to become Try (later Practice, now Learn; below).
- Learn is the main page (2026-10-27; before, Practice from 2026-10-23 and Try from 2026-10-22, and before that Learn's placeholder, with the classifier the developer tool `/dev/try`): `/learn`, where setup leads and returning visitors start. It runs step 5's small model (14 KB), logistic regression; the 11 MB k-NN can be chosen on the `/dev` dashboard (2026-10-23; before, a switch on Practice itself), only under `npm run dev`. The camera waits for a click there, so opening Sema never turns it on.
- Learn absorbed Practice (2026-10-27; Practice was Try renamed on 2026-10-23, since Try at `/try` was already what Practice was meant to be and Practice a placeholder): Learn and Practice had become nearly the same page, so Practice was merged into Learn; `/try` and `/practice` redirect to `/learn`, and the menu is Learn, Quiz, About. Learn starts at A; pick any letter in the guide, make it with the signing hand (Right by default, switched in the camera controls; since 2026-10-23 the other hand is neither drawn nor judged, and the status says "Show B with your right hand"; before, either hand counted), and hold it for 1.5 seconds while its finger conditions are met (until 2026-10-27, 3 seconds while it was Sema's sure guess); then "That's B!" with Next letter and Try again (before 2026-10-27 only Try again, and the next letter was picked by hand; until 2026-10-22 Try showed Sema's guess for every hand). The guide shows all 26 letters' drawings, as a list or a grid (J, which moves, was darkened and couldn't be checked until 2026-10-26, Z until 2026-10-24). Its layout is Record's (2026-10-23): the letter guide on the left, the camera with the Left | Right switch in the middle, and the letter on the right: its drawing large and its finger checklist (until 2026-10-27, its steps).
- Z is traced (2026-10-24; before, J and Z were both unavailable): Lanix's idea. Instead of recognizing the motion, which would need recordings of it and a model, Learn (Practice until 2026-10-27) draws a Z on the camera view (fixed in the frame; a right hand gets the mirrored Z) and the person traces it with the index fingertip, in order from point 1 to point 2 (reaching point 2 along the line is "That's Z!"), staying near the line, while keeping Z's handshape (index up, the other fingers curled, thumb across). A slip (off the line or out of the handshape) over 0.8 s starts it over; the line fills in as it's traced. No classifier is involved.
- J is traced (2026-10-26), like Z, at Lanix's request: the person first holds I (pinky up, the others curled, thumb across) for 1 second, then a curvy J appears with its start on the pinky tip (Lanix picked a swooping J, its hook coming back up past halfway, from three mockups), and they trace it with the pinky to point 2 while keeping the I. The J is as tall as the hand, wrist to pinky tip, measured as the I is held (Lanix's call: hands and pinkies differ, and a hand nearer the camera looks bigger), and the tracing tolerance grows and shrinks with it. A slip over 0.8 s removes the J, and it starts over from the I. No classifier is involved. (Tried and dropped the same day: no J to follow, judging the stroke's own turn, and then the wrist as a pivot.)
- Finger conditions decide (2026-10-27; before, the model's sure guess, with B's shape rules on top since 2026-10-23): every letter has finger conditions (`src/vision/landmarks/letterShapes.js`), each scored 0 to 1 from the hand's joint bends, thumb place and finger spread. The hold counts while they're met and no other still letter fits clearly better (`letterMatch.js`); Learn shows them as a checklist ticked live. The model is a second opinion on Learn (2026-10-29; before, only a "Right now" guess under `npm run dev`): its reading under the checklist, and after a pass whether it agreed over the hold. It never decides; a gate (pass only if the model agrees too) was considered and turned down, since it would fail correct signs on the model's weakest letters.

## Parked

- Phase 5, sign-in: optional and guest-first (leaning Supabase). Sema stays usable without an account.
- Phase 6, Filipino Sign Language: after the American version is finalized. It needs a verified source and a fluent reviewer.
- Which hand drives the left-hand flip: the detected hand or the signing-hand setting. Leaning toward the setting.
- What "Next letter" picks: the teaching order or the next unmastered letter. Decide in 3.3.
- When Practice unlocks (3 mastered letters was a starting guess, for 3.4): moot since 2026-10-27, when Practice was merged into Learn, where every letter is open.
- What "finalized" means: all 26 letters working, or some marked lower confidence. Leaning toward the second.
- The rest of 3.2: the top-bar hand pill's menu (Reset progress) and per-letter progress.

## Outside the plan

Ideas Lanix is building alongside the phases, not part of them: no phase waits on them.

- 3D hands (started 2026-10-31): a 2D | 3D switch on Learn's letter card shows the letter's
  pose on a 3D hand made in Lanix's own [Eidos](https://github.com/lanixiligan/eidos), instead
  of the drawing. 2D stays the default: 3D is opt-in for those who want it, so people who prefer
  reduced motion or need to save battery (phones, tablets) keep the drawings. Poses are
  in Eidos's format in `public/languages/asl/letters/`, all placeholders for now: Lanix fills
  them in. `docs/hand-poses.md`.
