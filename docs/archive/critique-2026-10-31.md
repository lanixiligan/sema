# Sema UI critique (2026-10-31)

Method: two separate reviews. A is a design review that looked at every main page in headless Chrome, light and dark, at 1440×900 and 390×844. B is Impeccable's automatic checker run on the code and on the live pages. A fake camera feed was used throughout and the real webcam was never opened. No project files were changed.

Target: the whole app (Welcome, About, Camera check, Learn, Quiz, Quiz run and results, Page not found). The developer pages were not reviewed.

## Design specificity verdict

**Design review (A):** Sema has real identity in four places:
- the ink-line **letter drawings**, its best asset;
- the **mark**, hand landmarks drawn as a constellation;
- the **finger checklist**, whose numbered badges match the fingertip numbers on the hand drawing;
- the **Page not found** page, which fingerspells L-O-S-T. It's the only moment where Sema's voice and its subject meet.

Everything else is a well-made but generic "calm product" kit that any webcam app could use unchanged: pill buttons on a sky wash, a centred hero of logo, sentence and button, About's 01/02/03 cards and shield badge, Quiz's trophy icon, a donut chart with a legend.

**Welcome has no hand on it at all**, and it's the first page anyone sees. The success moments ("That's B!", the 1 s quiz flash, the results) are plain text. Personality is missing exactly where feelings run highest (arrival, first pass, the end of a quiz), and appears only where nothing is at stake (Page not found).

**Automatic checker (B):** 2 code findings in `src/styles.css`:
- `overused-font` at :7 (Plus Jakarta Sans). Accurate, but a deliberate project choice.
- `bounce-easing` at :1305, the `.practice-done-title::before` tick pop. Accurate, and the reduced-motion rule at :1321 already turns it off.

In the browser:
- About: 6 findings, including two contrast failures and three "icon tile above heading" cards.
- Camera check: 3 "straggler" words left alone on the last line.
- Learn: 1 straggler.
- Quiz: only the font finding.
- Welcome and the running quiz: clean.

The checker found nothing on Welcome's main problem, its lack of character. That is a judgment no rule can make. It agrees with A that About is the most template-like page (the icon-tile cards).

## Overall impression

Sema is careful, trustworthy and calm. The camera ethics are excellent, and the Learn feedback loop is genuinely domain-specific. It doesn't yet feel like *Sema*, though: outside the drawings it could be any product. The biggest opportunity is to let the hand drawings and fingerspelling become the visual language of the moments around signing: arrival, success, results, idle and empty states. The signing surface itself stays calm.

## What's working

1. **The finger checklist with numbered badges** (1 thumb to 5 pinky) that match the overlay. It turns hidden model logic into "which finger is wrong".
2. **Camera ethics built into the experience.** The camera never starts by itself, Off is on every view, it stops when the tab is hidden or after an idle minute, and it says why. All of this goes through one shared `CameraView` / `CameraPanel`. Focus styles are visible on every focusable element (3px outline), and no page scrolls sideways on a phone.
3. **Page not found's L-O-S-T, and Quiz's honest peek eye** ("With a clue"). Both are playful, true to the product and never punishing. They prove the voice already exists.

## Priority issues

### [P1] Success and results moments have no character, and results go nowhere
- **Why it matters:** people remember the best moment and the last one. On a quiz where every letter was skipped, the results show a big **green "0"**, an orange ring and a wall of ten orange tiles, with no headline, no encouragement, and only one action, Start again. Learn's "That's A!" looks the same for the first letter as for the twenty-sixth.
- **Fix:**
  - Give the results a headline that depends on the score ("You fingerspelled 7 of 10" / "Tough round. These 3 need practice").
  - Colour the score number by result.
  - Show missed letters as drawing tiles that link to `/learn` with that letter selected.
  - On a pass, bring the letter's drawing in next to "That's B!".
- **Files:** `src/pages/quiz/QuizResults.jsx`, `src/pages/learn/LearnPage.jsx`, and `.practice-done` / `.quiz-score` in `styles.css`.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable delight`, `/impeccable onboard`

### [P1] Hands-up actions need the mouse
- **Why it matters:** the person's hands are in front of the camera. Next letter, Try again and Skip all need a click, and focus isn't moved to Next letter after a pass, so Space and Enter do nothing.
- **Fix:**
  - Focus Next letter when a pass happens.
  - Add Space (Next or Skip) with a small hint.
  - Consider moving on automatically in Learn after about 2 s, as the quiz does.
- **Files:** `LearnPage.jsx`, `QuizRun.jsx`.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable harden`

### [P2] Learn's right column: unclear order and wording
- **What:**
  - The checklist floats outside the card with no heading.
  - A shows "thumb" twice, both ①.
  - The "Letter model" box has nearly the same weight as the checklist, which is what actually decides. At the success moment it can even contradict the pass ("read U" under "That's V!").
- **Fix:**
  - Move `FingerChecklist` inside `.letter-card`, under a heading ("Your hand · 4 of 6").
  - Merge the two thumb conditions into one line.
  - Make `.model-opinion` quieter, or hide it behind a "What the model sees" toggle.
- **Also (from B):** Learn has **no `<h1>`** and the running quiz has no headings at all. All 26 guide tiles come before the camera in the Tab order (30+ presses) and there's no skip link.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable layout`, `/impeccable clarify`, `/impeccable audit`

### [P2] Welcome is the most generic page
- **What:** the logo appears twice (top bar and hero) and there are no hands, while the drawings are the most beautiful thing in the product.
- **Fix:** replace the hero logo with a fingerspelled greeting, e.g. "HI" or "SEMA" in drawing tiles. It's the Page not found tile pattern, which is already built (`.not-found-tile`, `AslDrawing`).
- **Commands:** `/impeccable delight`, `/impeccable bolder`

### [P2] Learn and Quiz place the camera differently
- **What:** the camera is in the centre column on Learn and on the left in Quiz, and the hand switch is in the camera controls on one and in a footer on the other. A person's eyes relearn where to look on every page change.
- **Fix:** use one camera position and one place for the controls on both pages.
- **Files:** the `.practice.learn` and `.quiz-run` grid areas.
- **Commands:** `/impeccable layout`

## Persona red flags

- **Jordan (first-timer):**
  - Welcome doesn't show what will happen.
  - The camera check asks for both hands without saying why, when Sema is signed with one.
  - Learn opens with 26 tiles, List/Grid, a hand switch and "Letter model is waiting for your right hand". Nothing says "start here: make A".
- **Sam (keyboard and screen reader):**
  - Learn has no h1, and the running quiz has no headings.
  - Tab goes through 26 tiles before reaching the camera.
  - The selected guide tile looks the same focused and unfocused.
  - After a pass, focus doesn't move.
  - The checklist ticks are 11px marks in small circles, with no larger "x of 6" summary.
  - About's 01/02/03 numbers have 1.3:1 contrast and aren't hidden from screen readers.
- **Casey (phone):**
  - The "made for computers" notice is honest.
  - After continuing, the camera is below the fold on Learn, Quiz and the camera check.
  - The theme toggle takes half the top bar.
  - Many tap targets are under 44px: the theme options (38×30), the camera menu (32×44), List/Grid (42×26), End quiz (95×40).
- **A hearing learner fingerspelling for a Deaf family member (motivated, worried about getting it wrong):**
  - Wants to know a pass means *correct ASL*.
  - Is unsettled when the model disagrees at the success moment.
  - Wants help with look-alikes (A/S/E/M/N/T).
  - Wants to feel progress ("I know 14 letters").
  - Reads the orange results wall as failure.

## Minor observations

- About:
  - The step numbers `span.intro-step-number` are #C9E6F3 on white, 1.3:1. If they're decoration, add `aria-hidden`; otherwise darken them.
  - `span.intro-key` is 4.4:1 on the gradient.
  - The "Handshape B" sketch in the About hero shows a spread hand with the thumb out, which doesn't match B. A learner will notice.
- Quiz:
  - The "Letters" card says "Practice 10 random letters".
  - The "Start" pill inside each clickable card looks like a separate button.
  - The results' Skipped swatch has no icon, while the other two do. That matters for colour-blind people, since the ring is green and orange.
  - The camera header repeats "Show E…" next to a giant E.
- Camera check:
  - Three paragraphs end with a single word on their last line.
  - Continue sits at the bottom edge at a 900px window height.
- Learn:
  - "Letter model is waiting for your right / hand" breaks before its last word.
  - The empty dashed `model-tile` reads as a broken image.
  - In dark theme the white drawing tiles glare slightly.
- About's "How it works" band is very tall and sparse at 1440×900.
- About's size scale is the loosest in the app: 13 font sizes on one page (Welcome uses 4, Learn 9).

## Questions to consider

1. If the finger rules decide and the model never does, why show the model to learners at the success moment, where it can only create doubt?
2. What if every pass were *spelled back*: the drawing of the letter just made slotting into a growing strip of today's letters?
3. Why does Welcome show the logo twice and no hands?
4. Should a Words quiz end with the word itself, fingerspelled in drawings?
5. Is A to Z the right first lesson for a nervous learner, or should the first minute be a three-letter win, such as their own name?

## Where personality could live

These are ranked. All of them stay off the camera while someone is signing.

1. **Fingerspelled headlines**, reusing Page not found's tile pattern: "HI" on Welcome, "OK" in the camera check's ready flash, "NICE" or the finished word on the quiz results.
2. **A "today" strip on Learn:** small drawing tiles that fill in as letters pass. It gives progress a shape, and works with saved progress later.
3. **The pass moment:** the letter's tile springs in next to "That's B!" and the hand overlay's bones flash the accent colour, then settle. About 600 ms, reduced motion respected, only after the hold.
4. **The mark as a status:** the constellation dots breathe while the hand model loads, and in Camera is off.
5. **A coach's voice** in `letterHold.js`, `CameraOff` and `IdleNotice`: "Still there? The camera rests in 30 s." After about 15 s without a pass, one hint aimed at the closest unmet condition ("Almost: tuck your thumb against your fist").
6. **Results that teach:** missed letters as drawing tiles with "Practise these".
7. **Look-alike notes** in Learn's letter card: "A, S, E: watch the thumb", with two mini drawings.
8. **About's hero:** the real B drawing with the 21 tracking points over it, replacing the sketch that doesn't match B.
9. **Quiz choice cards:** the sample letters as tiny drawings, and the mark instead of the trophy.
10. **A values note on About:** what fingerspelling is for, and that Sema adds to Deaf teachers rather than replacing them.
